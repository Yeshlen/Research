\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: amount}}\\
amount & Voucher amount (R) & Scammers push towards the limit \\
amount\_ratio\_to\_avg\_90d & Amount $\div$ customer's mean voucher in the last 90 days & Unusual for this customer \\
amount\_ratio\_to\_max & Amount $\div$ customer's largest earlier voucher & Larger than anything sent before \\
amount\_to\_limit\_ratio & Amount $\div$ current daily limit & Draining the full limit \\
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: history and velocity}}\\
history\_count & Number of earlier vouchers & First-time use; reliability of the baseline \\
has\_history$^\dagger$ & Any earlier voucher (0/1) & Duplicates \texttt{history\_count} \\
days\_since\_last\_voucher & Days since the previous voucher & Dormant customer suddenly active \\
vouchers\_24h, amount\_24h, vouchers\_7d & Earlier vouchers and value in rolling windows & Velocity \\
vouchers\_in\_session & Earlier vouchers in the same app session & Split-voucher scams \\
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: recipient}}\\
recipient\_known & Recipient used in an earlier voucher (0/1) & New beneficiary \\
is\_self\_send & Voucher sent to the customer's own number (0/1) & ``Send it to yourself'' scam \\
recipient\_added\_in\_session & Recipient added in this session (0/1) & Beneficiary added under pressure \\
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: session behaviour}}\\
secs\_since\_login & Seconds from login to voucher creation & Rushed or coached sessions \\
screens\_before\_create & App screens viewed before creation & Victim guided through the app \\
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: timing}}\\
hour$^\dagger$, day\_of\_week$^\dagger$ & Time of creation & Calendar context \\
hour\_deviation & Circular distance from the customer's usual hour & Unusual time for this customer \\
is\_night & Created between 21:00 and 06:00 (0/1) & Unusual time in general \\
is\_payday\_window$^\dagger$ & Created between the 25th and the 1st (0/1) & Explains legitimate peaks \\
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage A: account changes}}\\
limit\_changed\_24h, limit\_increase\_24h & Limit changed in the last 24 h, and by how much & Victims coached to raise limits \\
days\_since\_device\_change, new\_device\_7d & Recency of a new device & Account takeover overlap \\
password\_reset\_7d & Password reset in the last 7 days (0/1) & Credential change before payout \\
account\_age\_days & Age of the account & New accounts \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Stage B: redemption}}\\
redeemed$^\dagger$ & Voucher cashed out within the window & Stage B exists only if redeemed \\
redeem\_delay\_min & Minutes from creation to cash-out & Fraudster waiting to collect \\
redeem\_dist\_home\_km & Distance of cash-out point from home & Cash-out far away \\
redeem\_dist\_usual\_km & Distance from centroid of earlier cash-outs & Outside the usual area \\
redeem\_hour$^\dagger$, redeem\_is\_night & Time of cash-out & Unusual cash-out time \\
redeem\_at\_atm & ATM rather than retailer (0/1) & Cash-out channel \\
